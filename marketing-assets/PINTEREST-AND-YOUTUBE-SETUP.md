# PART 2 — YOUTUBE

## The honest assessment first

**You have 31 procedure videos in the asset drop** — basement steps, washer gasket, shower head, mirror, toaster oven, fridge drip tray, and so on. They're well-shot and they'd make excellent silent how-to Shorts, which is the strongest possible YouTube format for a non-camera-facing brand.

**But they were filmed inside a client's home.**

Publishing client household footage without explicit written permission is a serious problem for an organizing business, and it's specifically at odds with the confidentiality positioning in the luxury article. Even without a face or a house number, interiors are identifiable to anyone who knows the family.

**So before any of that footage goes near YouTube, you need written permission from the homeowner, naming YouTube specifically.** If that isn't available, the footage stays internal to the SOP library where it belongs.

**The alternative that carries no risk:** film the same procedures in your own home, or in a client space with permission arranged in advance. The techniques are yours; the footage doesn't have to be theirs.
