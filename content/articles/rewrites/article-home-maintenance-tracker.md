# Home Maintenance Tracker: Never Forget A Task Again

Nobody forgets to change the HVAC filter because they don't care. They forget because nothing tells them.

Home maintenance is the category with no natural reminder. The dishes announce themselves. The dryer vent doesn't — right up until it does.

---

## Why it needs writing down

Everything else in a house has a signal. Laundry piles up. The fridge empties. Someone asks where their shoes are.

Maintenance is silent. The filter gets dirty invisibly, the gutters fill invisibly, the smoke alarm ages invisibly. **By the time there's a signal, it's a repair, not maintenance** — and that's the whole difference in cost.

A tracker isn't about being organized. It's about creating a signal where the house doesn't give you one.

---

## What to track

**Monthly.** HVAC filter check. Test smoke and carbon monoxide alarms. Run water in any drain nobody uses.

**Quarterly.** Change the HVAC filter — check your own filter's packaging, intervals vary a lot by type. Clear the dryer vent. Check under every sink for leaks.

**Twice yearly.** Gutters. Water heater. Alarm batteries. Walk the exterior looking for water.

**Annually.** HVAC service. Chimney inspection if you have one, which the NFPA and CSIA both recommend yearly. Septic if applicable. Reseal anything that needs it.

**Once, then whenever it changes.** Every appliance's brand, model, serial number and age. HVAC filter size. Fridge filter model.

**Photograph the model plate on each appliance.** It's always on a sticker behind something, and every repair call and part order starts with that number.

---

## The two that actually matter

**Dryer lint.** The NFPA finds failure to clean is the leading contributing factor in home clothes-dryer fires — about a third of them — and that dust, fibre and lint are the most common first item ignited. Clean the screen every load and have the vent cleared yearly.

**Smoke alarms have a service life.** Around ten years, regardless of battery changes, per NFPA guidance. Most people have never checked the manufacture date on theirs. It's printed on the back. Go and look.

Everything else on the list saves money. Those two are different.

---

## Where to keep it

Wherever you'll actually see it. A calendar with recurring reminders beats a beautiful spreadsheet nobody opens.

**The test is whether it interrupts you.** A list you have to remember to check has the same problem as remembering the maintenance itself.

Set the recurring reminders once. Then it's not your memory's job any more.

---

## Start with four

Not the full list. Four reminders, today:

- HVAC filter, quarterly
- Dryer vent, yearly
- Smoke alarm test, monthly
- Gutters, twice yearly

That's most of the risk and most of the cost, in about five minutes of setup. Add the rest as you go.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do people forget home maintenance?

Because it's the only household category with no natural signal. Laundry piles up, the fridge empties, but a filter gets dirty invisibly and a dryer vent fills invisibly. By the time there's a signal it's a repair rather than maintenance, which is where the cost is.

### What home maintenance actually matters most?

Dryer lint and smoke alarms. The NFPA finds failure to clean is the leading contributing factor in home clothes-dryer fires, roughly a third of them, and smoke alarms have a service life of about ten years regardless of battery changes. Most people have never checked the date printed on the back of theirs.

### How often should I change my HVAC filter?

Check the packaging on your own filter — intervals vary considerably by filter type, and any single figure quoted online is likely wrong for yours. Check monthly, change quarterly as a starting point, and adjust to what your filter actually specifies.

### Where should I keep a maintenance tracker?

Somewhere that interrupts you. A calendar with recurring reminders works because it comes to you; a spreadsheet fails because you have to remember to open it, which is the same problem as remembering the maintenance.
